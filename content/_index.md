<p class="home-lede">The United States Internet Preservation Society operates Internet number resources and network infrastructure in furtherance of its 501(c)(4) purpose: preserving open, private, and impartial access to the Internet.</p>

This site, usips.net, documents how the USIPS network is operated: who we are, what we run, what we permit, and what happens when you send us a complaint. Our advocacy and membership site is [usips.org](https://usips.org).

Abuse reports sent to <abuse@usips.net> are read and answered by a human within 24 hours, barring exceptional circumstances. Every report receives a written disposition. Where the answer is no action, the disposition states why.
