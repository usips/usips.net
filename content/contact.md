Role addresses, not personal mailboxes. Every address below is delivered to the responsible officers; none terminates in a single unmonitored inbox. Use the subject-line tags to speed routing.

## Role addresses

| Address | Handles | Subject tag |
|---|---|---|
| <abuse@usips.net> | Abuse reports, AUP enforcement intake ([how to report](/abuse/)) | `[ABUSE]` |
| <noc@usips.net> | Operational escalation, peering/routing issues, LOA verification, AUP appeals | `[NOC]`, `[APPEAL]` |
| <security@usips.net> | Vulnerability reports, [security.txt](/.well-known/security.txt) contact | `[SECURITY]` |
| <legal@usips.net> | Legal process, law enforcement correspondence ([guide](/legal/)) | `[LEGAL]` |
| <dmca@usips.net> | Copies of DMCA notices (designated agent: [see Legal](/legal/)) | `[DMCA]` |
| <ncii@usips.net> | Nonconsensual intimate imagery removal requests | `[NCII]` |
| <peering@usips.net> | Peering requests ([policy](/network/)) | `[PEERING]` |
| <privacy@usips.net> | Data subject requests ([policy](/privacy/)) | `[PRIVACY]` |

Acknowledgment target for abuse reports: a human within 24 hours, barring exceptional circumstances. Other addresses are answered in the order received.
